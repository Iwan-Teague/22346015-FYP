## 3. Requirements only the harness can meet (not prompts)

Every **[HARNESS-ONLY]** item above is unachievable by prompt text, because each one failed *while its prompt-side rule already existed*. Concretely, the harness must own: (1) artifact content validation (H-01, H-02) — prompts cannot inspect bytes; (2) measurement of verification (exit codes, refusal witnesses, timeouts — H-03, H-04, H-17, H-24) — the agent under test cannot be its own measurement device (threat model: control plane trusts agent-forgeable proxies, `agent-fleet-threat-model-v0.1.md:155-166` attack path 7); (3) write confinement and secret brokering (H-06, H-07) — the read-only rule was prose and was violated (AQ-152 plus the live `OUTDIR/` instance this session); (4) payload transport without shell/argv (H-08) — wave-16 corruption happened in the launcher, outside the agent's reach; (5) run identity, lifecycle, and recovery (H-09–H-14) — liveness/done-ness mistakes were all made by the supervisor, not the lanes; (6) attribution (H-15) — porcelain cannot attribute, only a snapshotting harness can; (7) claim structuring (H-18–H-21) — an agent's free-text assertion is exactly what failed in AQ-143/146/129; (8) governance transitions (H-22, H-23) — two-eyes and two-step stamps were both bypassed in practice. Prompt-side rules remain necessary for legibility, but by this fleet's own record they are necessary-and-insufficient: the load-bearing subset is harness-enforced.
